[h1]Riftbound Mod TTS Ogy[/h1]
A free, unofficial Tabletop Simulator table for the Riftbound trading card game. Paste your Piltover Archive deck code and your whole deck lays itself out – legend, champion, runes, battlefields, sideboard and tokens.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]1v1, 2v2, FFA 3 and FFA 4[/b] on one table, every player with their own mat and table side
[*][b]Deck-code import[/b] from Piltover Archive, with card images and text from Riot's official card gallery (all sets)
[*][b]Automatic setup:[/b] everything goes to its zone; pick your champion privately from your hand
[*][b]Official game flow:[/b] random first player, battlefields per mode, opening hand, mulligan, turn phases, Burn Out, first-turn rules
[*][b]Peek and reveal[/b] with quick action buttons (top / bottom of deck, trash, banish, to hand)
[*][b]Drag to TOP or BOTTOM[/b] of your main deck and rune deck
[*][b]Supply:[/b] Buff, Might, Damage, Stunned and Tag markers, dice, and a token card selector (Recruit, Sprite, Gold, …)
[*][b]Battlefield text[/b] printed on the table, readable from every seat
[*]All controls are on the table itself – no screen overlay
[/list]

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Press [b]Sit here[/b] on the control strip at your side of the table.
[*]Host picks the mode on the host console.
[*]Paste your deck code into your strip and press [b]Import[/b]. Drag one champion from your hand onto the Champion slot.
[*]Host presses [b]Start game[/b]. Mulligan by dragging up to 2 cards back onto your deck, then press [b]Mulligan done[/b].
[/olist]
The in-game notebook and [b]!rb help[/b] in chat explain the rest.

[h2]Bugs and ideas[/h2]
Press [b]Report a bug[/b] on the host console, type [b]!rb bug[/b] in chat, or open an issue on GitHub:
[url=https://github.com/Ogy60/Riftbound-Mod-TTS-Ogy/issues]github.com/Ogy60/Riftbound-Mod-TTS-Ogy[/url]
Please include your deck code, the mode and any chat line starting with [Riftbound].
